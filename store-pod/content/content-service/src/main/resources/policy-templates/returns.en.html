Return window

You may return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

How to return

Contact us with your order number; we will confirm the return address and, where applicable, send a label.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 days of receiving the return. Outbound shipping is refunded when the item was faulty or not as described.

Exceptions

Perishable, personalised and hygiene-sealed items cannot be returned once opened unless faulty.
