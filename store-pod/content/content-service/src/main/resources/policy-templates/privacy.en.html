What we collect

Your name, contact details, delivery address, order history and the technical data needed to run the store (device, browser, IP address).

Why

To fulfil your orders, provide support, prevent fraud and — where you agree — send you updates about products and offers.

Who we share it with

Payment providers, carriers and the service providers that host and run this store, each bound to process it only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account is active and as required by tax and accounting law afterwards.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, object to marketing and lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.
