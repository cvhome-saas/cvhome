# pink — Tokyo Girls Issue

The storefront theme for a **mixed girly-lifestyle merchant** selling to teenage girls and young women in
Japanese girls' visual culture: apparel, accessories, beauty and stationery in one catalogue.

The shop is **this month's issue of a Japanese girls' fashion magazine**. Whole regions flood with the
merchant's PRIMARY the way a cover floods with ink; every product is a die-cut ranged in a ruled cell with
its price printed on a notched flag in the merchant's ACCENT; the sections of the issue are numbered and
the masthead always states where you are; sale, low stock and sold out are marker annotations drawn over
the goods. Dela Gothic One names, prices and flags; M PLUS Rounded 1c explains; Cairo leads Arabic per
glyph. One authored motion: an ADDED flag snaps onto a cell when the basket's count actually rises.

the contract is the comment at the top of `src/layout/Root.tsx`.
